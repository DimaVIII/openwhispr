## Configuration and acceptance sequence

1. The account owner identifies the intended Play developer account/app and grants the necessary existing-account access, or completes the provider configuration. Do not create a second developer account to work around the current signup screen. Obtain access to the intended Expo project as well. RevenueCat's current role cannot add app configurations.
2. Confirm the actual Play application ID, subscription products, base plans, offers, regions and license testers. Use their approved values; this audit does not select new prices, invent product IDs or authorize purchases. Check whether prepaid plans are present before treating pending prepaid payment as a reachable acceptance case.
3. Connect that Play app and its products to the intended RevenueCat project with the required store credentials and backend entitlement integration. Preserve existing App Store/web/workspace access. RevenueCat remains the sole Android purchaser; Superwall supplies presentation. A successful SDK purchase must still reconcile to the signed-in account's server access.
4. Configure the Superwall Android package and corresponding Play products, then prepare the intended Android paywall/campaign against the actual app placements. `src/lib/superwall.ts` is the source of truth: `onboarding_paywall`, `account_billing_open`, `cloud_usage_warning_tapped`, `cloud_usage_limit_reached`, `cloud_sync_required`, `cloud_transcription_start`, `audio_upload_start`, `note_dictation_start`, `ai_action_run`, `note_chat_start`, `meeting_record_start`, and `voice_profile_additional_speaker`. Confirm which are reachable on Android and their intended audience/access behavior; do not publish the example campaign as a substitute. Remotely managed presentation must not introduce a second purchase controller.
5. Supply the Android public SDK keys through the approved build environment: `EXPO_PUBLIC_SUPERWALL_ANDROID_API_KEY` and `EXPO_PUBLIC_REVENUECAT_ANDROID_API_KEY`. Inspect both Expo project and account environments without exporting secret values. The local review environment currently has neither Android key. Do not substitute an iOS or Test Store key.
6. Build the correctly identified Android app for authorized Play test distribution, then use a configured license tester to verify store-loaded prices, cancellation, successful purchase, backend access, restore, reinstall and account changes. Test pending-payment recovery where supported. Preserve unresolved purchases unless authoritative original-transaction evidence resolves them. The installed SDK's missing original pending token and the backend's single selected store source remain separate implementation/integration limits.

The v20 emulator APK and its source are unchanged by this audit. The emulator remains normally PIN-locked; no new foreground, microphone, purchase or restore acceptance is claimed. Physical phone benchmarks, current spoken flows and the other gaps in [review readiness](premium-review-readiness.md) remain open.
